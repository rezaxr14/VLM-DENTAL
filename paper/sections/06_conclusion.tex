\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\sys{} makes grounding correction an explicit action in a dental VLM pipeline. Box-relative
revision, deliberately imperfect demonstration-time grounding, and a correction-friendly reward
together define a testable approach to learning when to look again. The completed corpus
analysis shows a teacher correction gradient across perturbation tiers; it does not show that
the trained student corrects errors or improves diagnostic accuracy. Those conclusions require
the pending SFT/GRPO comparisons under the frozen protocol.

The inference extension separates an exact rules rendering from on-demand procedural skills,
and the serving extension tests speculative decoding against the same numerical target. Their
quality and efficiency results are reported independently, with missing measurements left
explicit. Together with the released sources and audit trail, this separation keeps both
positive and negative results interpretable. The next step is to complete the predefined
trained-policy comparisons, followed by broader external validation.
